# sales-dashboard-project
Sales Forecasting &amp; Business Strategy Analysis
🔍 Problem
Businesses need accurate sales forecasts to optimize inventory, plan resources, and avoid losses due to overstocking or understocking.
📁 Dataset
Time-based sales dataset containing:
Date
Sales Revenue
Product / Category (if available)
⚙️ Tools Used
Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn)
Matplotlib / Seaborn
📊 Model Overview
A Linear Regression model was used to forecast future sales based on historical trends.
📉 Forecast Visualization

📈 Key Insights
Upward sales trend detected, indicating consistent business growth over time
Model captures overall trend effectively, though minor deviations exist in short-term fluctuations
Seasonal variations (if visible) suggest recurring patterns that can be leveraged for planning
Prediction gap highlights volatility, indicating external factors may influence sales
💡 Business Impact
Enables proactive inventory planning and demand forecasting
Helps reduce losses from overstocking or stockouts
Supports strategic decision-making for future growth
Assists in identifying trends for marketing and sales planning
📌 Recommendations
Increase inventory ahead of predicted high-demand periods
Investigate causes of prediction deviations (external factors, seasonality)
Combine forecasting with external data (holidays, campaigns) for better accuracy
